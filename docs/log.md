# Build Log

## 2026-10-29 (Week 1: BOM and order)

Done
- Ordered every part on the BOM from Amazon.ca. Estimated total was $130 to $180 CAD.
- Arduino Uno REV3 (genuine, not a clone)
- CNC Shield V3, 2 pack. Using 1, the other is a spare.
- A4988 stepper drivers, 4 pack. Using 2, so I have 2 spares if one burns out.
- 2x NEMA17 stepper motors. Picked the 3401S version over the 4401S.
- GT2 timing belt and GT2 pulleys (5 pack, using 2)
- 4x smooth steel rods, 8mm by 300mm (2 packs of 2)
- SG90 micro servo kit for the pen lift
- MDF boards, 1/4 inch, 11x14, 10 pack. Plan is to glue two sheets per panel to get close to 12mm.
- 12V 2A power supply
- Breadboard and jumper wires (3 pack, using 1)
- M3 screw and nut kit (420 pieces) and a 100 pack of zip ties
- USB A to USB B cable

Decisions
- Went with MDF for the frame instead of 3D printed or acrylic parts. Laminating two 1/4 inch sheets gets the thickness I need from a cheap 10 pack.
- Bought multipacks for the shield and drivers because the spares cost almost nothing extra and cover me if I fry one during wiring.

Open items
- Parts not delivered yet. Week 2 frame build depends on the MDF and rods showing up.
